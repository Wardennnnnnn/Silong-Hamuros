# Weather guidance

The v0.3.1 interface uses plain English labels, larger bold measurements with aligned digits, a prominent rain-probability card, and separate icon cards for drying today, suggested start, and cover reminders. Supporting explanations are grouped under an expandable “About this guide” section. Brown and green remain the core theme; amber identifies rain-risk reminders, with text labels alongside color.

The phone now fetches real Open-Meteo forecasts directly over HTTPS, independently of the ESP32/backend. The default is an approximate Lipa City reference (13.9411, 121.1631), obtained from Open-Meteo geocoding. Replace it with confirmed farm coordinates in Settings. No location permission is needed for this saved-location approach.

Weather shows hourly rain probability, a 24-hour strip, suggested drying windows today, a suggested start window across three forecast days, and a cover preparation reminder 30 minutes before the first risky hourly interval. All forecast times are displayed in Philippine time, regardless of phone timezone. Accumulated hourly precipitation, probability, and radiation timestamps refer to the preceding hour; interval labels reflect this.

Experimental favorable-window rules: at least two remaining consecutive daylight hours, probability below 30%, precipitation below 0.1 mm/hour, RH below 80%, radiation at least 150 W/m², and clear/cloudy weather codes 0–3. Rank windows by summed radiation × (1 − probability/100) × (1 − humidity/100). This ranks individual windows, not whole-day or full-batch drying performance. Rules are heuristics requiring farm validation; they are not a validated agronomic model. Rain reminders use the configured probability threshold OR predicted precipitation >= 0.1 mm/hour OR thunderstorm code. The reminder is preparation advice, not an exact rain onset prediction, and never sends a motor command.

Refresh weather on launch, every 30 minutes while the app is running, or manually. Failed requests retry no sooner than five minutes automatically. Cached forecasts older than one hour show as outdated and do not produce recommendations. Changing locations clears incompatible weather. Reminders are in-app only; background notifications are not implemented. No hardware measurements are generated by this feature.
